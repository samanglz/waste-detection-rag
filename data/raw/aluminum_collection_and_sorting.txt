Aluminum Recycling and Collection Practices

Aluminum cans and aluminum foil can be recyclable where local
programs accept them. Food residue should be removed from foil
before recycling, according to the relevant collection instructions.
Metal items are sorted and processed before the recovered material
is used to manufacture new products.

Whether an aluminum can should be crushed depends on the collection
and sorting system. In some single-stream systems, cans are identified
by sorting equipment, and crushing them can make detection more
difficult. In other systems, crushing may be permitted. Because
collection systems differ, residents should follow their provider's
specific preparation instructions rather than assume one rule applies
everywhere.

Metal recycling also includes different categories of materials.
Ferrous metals contain iron and can often be separated using magnets.
Aluminum is a non-ferrous metal and is not separated by ordinary
magnetic attraction in the same way as iron-containing materials.
Sorting methods depend on the materials and equipment handled by
the processing facility.

Correct separation and preparation help facilities recover materials
that can be used again, but local acceptance rules remain decisive.
